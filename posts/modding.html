Minecraft Modding

January 17, 2026

happy very late new year i guess?

this isn’t gonna be a typical blog post i make here, this is just me going over my two new minecraft mods i’ve made, which are my nugget mod and my more spear enchantments mod.

my first mod, nugget, was the first minecraft mod i’ve ever made. i made it because i didn’t really have any other ideas for my first mod and i thought it was funny (which it definitely still is).

it’s literally just a chicken nugget with the roblox man face as its face. i added armor, tools and weapons, armor trims and materials, a custom nugget ore (raw and normal), and raw nugget because why not.

i first started working on it back on December 14, 2024. anyways, it has gathered about 535 downloads so far on curseforge (since Jan 17, 2026).

my second mod is the more spear enchantments mod, which is my more recent one. it adds more enchantments to spears, and adds three new enchantments in total.

that’s probably all i’m gonna add here

both mods support fabric, neoforge, and forge for versions 1.21.11+
